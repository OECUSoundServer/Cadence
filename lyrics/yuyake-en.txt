Swaying my tail, gently caught in the breeze, Drifting with ease, doing just as I please.
My ears perk at the soft wind, Amber light lingers, pulls me in.
Clouds glow gold, fold and unfold, Low lazy yawn as calm takes hold.
Soft air slips light through my fur, My tail trails low, slow curling curves.

Soft breezes tease, tail sways slow, Easy winds weave where I wander and go so slow.
On the rooftop, I roll down low, Watching the world in the warm amber glow.
Sun-warm fur still slowly resting in the afterglow haze glow.
Gaze at the softly glowing amber skies for quite a while there.

Whenever I please in the sunset glow so slow and low now,
Hop past the breeze in a light little flow still slow.
By pure instinct, I set my sight just right in time,
Eyes fixed steady, intent and aligned.

Softly I wash, brushing over my face slow and calm,
So small gentle sigh as I loosen my pace and flow slow.
Sunset skies softly melt into fading dream,
Slowly dissolving in warm amber gleam glow.


Tail drifting lightly, I stretch out easy, easing into calm smooth motion.
Traces of sleep still linger, slowly leaving my drowsy eyes.
Softly I rub and brush at my still-drowsy half awake eyes.
Getting ready now to wander where I roam on my own free.

Chasing soft floating clouds drifting through the quiet open breaks in the glowing sky.
Walking along a softly sparkling little glowing quiet path.
Sky colors slowly shifting and softly rearranging their tone.
Tomorrow again beneath the same wide sky waiting for me.
